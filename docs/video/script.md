# Keel demo video script

Video: https://youtu.be/iSvGuXuLK7A

**Hook**

In June, forty nine people on Mezo had a MUSD loan against their Bitcoin. Today almost none are left. They didn't repay. They were redeemed.

**Hero**

This is Keel. Borrow against your Bitcoin, and never get redeemed.

**Problem**

On Mezo today, when MUSD trades under a dollar, anyone can buy it cheap and swap it for the Bitcoin in the weakest loan. And MUSD has sat under that line since July.

**How it works**

Keel moves the loan where redemptions can't reach. Savers fund it from Mezo's savings vault. You lock Bitcoin or a veBTC lock, borrow MUSD, and your interest pays the savers.

**Quote**

Let's take one. I lock a little test Bitcoin and ask for fifty MUSD. Before signing, I see what I can borrow, the rate, my health, and the line that matters. Redemptions can't take it.

**Borrow**

I connect from the top bar, approve, and borrow. The MUSD is in my wallet.

**Loan**

Here's my loan and its health. Only a deep fall in Bitcoin could liquidate it, and anything left over comes back to me.

**Repay**

I can repay any time. The interest goes to savers, and my Bitcoin comes straight back.

**Savers**

We ran the saver side too. Two savers each put in sixty MUSD, and each took out a little more than they put in.

**Under the hood**

Under the hood, Keel is a strategy for Mezo's own savings vault, where millions of MUSD sit idle. One call from the vault's owner plugs it in, and we tested that call on a copy of Mezo mainnet.

**Explorer**

None of this is simulated. Every step you just saw is a real transaction on Mezo testnet.

**Close**
