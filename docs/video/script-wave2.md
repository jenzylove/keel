# Keel, Wave 2 demo video script

About 1:45. Recorded on the live site; the Mezo loan move is a real testnet transaction made on camera.

**hook**

In June, forty nine people on Mezo had a MUSD loan against their Bitcoin. By October, one was left. They didn't repay. They were redeemed.

**new**

In wave one, Keel gave new borrowers a safe place. This wave, Keel goes after the loans Mezo already has. Three things are new.

**move1**

Here's a wallet with a real Mezo loan on testnet. Redemptions can reach it. Keel reads it and offers a move: the same Bitcoin, the same debt, and no MUSD needed.

**move2**

One signature lets Mezo close the loan for Keel. Keel pays it off with savers' MUSD, Mezo sends the Bitcoin across, and the same debt opens again in Keel.

**moved**

Done. The Mezo loan is gone, and the same debt now lives in Keel, where redemptions can't reach it.

**movetx**

Here's that move on Mezo's explorer. One transaction, start to finish.

**loop**

Second, locked Bitcoin pays its own loan. A veBTC lock keeps voting for a Mezo pool. Every Thursday the pool pays its voters. Keel collects that share, swaps any Bitcoin into MUSD on Mezo's own pool, and the debt drops.

**paid**

This is the first real payment. The lock's rewards came in, and the loan's debt went down. Nobody touched it.

**mainnet**

Third, Keel runs on Mezo mainnet with real money. A forty eight hour timelock owns the vault, and a keeper checks every loan each day.

**close**

Keel. Borrow against your Bitcoin, and never get redeemed. Try it at keel-psi-seven.vercel.app.
